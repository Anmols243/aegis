A suspicious email arrives at the AEGIS inbox. Six agents read it — triage, forensics, vision, sandbox, threat-intel, arbiter — and each one has to cite its evidence. This is the case file for the first live run.

Just over the scam line — and the verdict says so. The dissent log records the disagreement: forensic scored 0.97, the sandbox could not resolve the domain and scored 0.30. No black boxes.

Evidence

Exhibit A

Homoglyph in the sender name

The display name reads "PayPaI Security" — capital I where the second l should be. The sending domain is paypa1-secure.com, with the digit 1 standing in for l. Neither matches paypal.com.

Exhibit B

Credential-harvesting URL

http://paypa1-secure.com/verify?session=8f3k2 — plain HTTP, a lookalike host, and a /verify path built to collect logins. The sandbox could not resolve the domain, so it scored the link as unreachable rather than safe — the verdict does not treat "couldn't load" as "clean".

Exhibit C

Urgency as a weapon

"limited within 24 hours", "verify your identity immediately", "permanent suspension" — three escalating threats in five lines. Authentication headers failed across the board: spf=fail; dkim=fail; dmarc=fail.
